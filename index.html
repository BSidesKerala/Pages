Welcome to Security BSides Kerala 2027

The hacking and cyber security community conference. The perfect collaboration of hackers, makers, security professionals and security industry leaders.

Tickets and CFP opening soon. This is the third edition of BSides Kerala.

A message from Jack Daniel, co-founder of Security BSides

Jack Daniel is a storyteller, blacksmith, luthier, woodcarver, comic, bartender, and motorcyclist. In other words, Jack is retired. Jack is also a community builder and historian, he is a co-founder of Security BSides and the creator of the Shoulders of InfoSec Project.

In a past life, he added things like technologist, security professional, admin, podcaster, and blogger to his bio, but no longer. Jack also used to list things like jobs, such as the many he held at Tenable before retiring.

Tracks and events

Nine spaces to move between across the conference, whatever your level of experience.

  • CXO Track

    Security strategy, AI, critical infrastructure and supply chain risk for the people who set direction.

  • Hacker Ground

    Deep technical talks and live demos from practitioners, covering offensive research, tooling and incident response.

  • Rookie Track

    A friendly room for first-time speakers and people new to the field, with mentoring along the way.

  • Trainings

    Two full days of instructor-led courses ahead of the conference, diving deep into offensive and defensive skills.

  • Workshop

    Focused, interactive sessions where you build, break and test tools alongside practitioners.

  • Hands-on Activity

    Hardware, IoT and wireless labs, lockpicking and live hacking demos you take part in yourself.

  • CTF

    The on-site capture-the-flag, with challenges modelled on real-world scenarios.

  • Career Space

    Get your CV looked over and talk to people doing the job you want next.

  • Expo

    Explore sponsor booths, community villages and product demos across the conference floor.
