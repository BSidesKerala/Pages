About BSides Kerala

Hacking and Cyber Security Community Conference

BSides Kerala is an open, community-run cyber security conference where learning and sharing come first. The programme is technical: talks and hands-on workshops from people working in the field.

The audience runs from students to people twenty years into the job, and the tracks are pitched for both. The organising team are security practitioners and hackers who put the event together in their own time.
